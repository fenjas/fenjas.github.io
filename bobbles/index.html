How to play

  • Tap to drop the next Bobble into the jar
  • Two matching Bobbles merge into the next size up
  • Merge a creature and a plant of the same colour to summon a rare hybrid
  • Merge again quickly to build combos
  • Keep everything inside the jar. If it overflows, the run is over

Why you'll love it

  • Real physics: Bobbles bounce, squash, roll and settle
  • Googly eyes that blink, glance around and react to every bump
  • Rare hybrid creatures to discover, from the tiny Mossling to the mighty Verdant Dragon
  • Paper planes every 20,000 points to clear a crowded jar
  • Missions that make every run feel different
  • Your run is saved, so you can pick up where you left off
  • Plays offline, no account needed

Ads and purchases

Bobbles is free to play. It shows ads between some runs, and you can watch an optional ad for a second chance. A one-time purchase removes the between-run ads for good.
