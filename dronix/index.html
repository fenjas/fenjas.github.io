Dronix

Shift colours, absorb enemy fire and blast neon drone swarms in a one-thumb arcade shooter. Chain combos up to x8, unleash Overdrive and take down guardian bosses.

  • Arcade
  • Shooter
  • 30 levels + Endless
  • Plays offline

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to fly. Your drone fires automatically
  • Tap to shift between cyan, magenta and amber
  • Absorb shots that match your colour, dodge the rest
  • Match enemy colours for double damage and a CHAIN up to x8
  • Fill the meter and tap OVERDRIVE for five seconds of rainbow firepower

Why you'll love it

  • One-thumb controls: easy to pick up, hard to master
  • 30 levels across 6 neon sectors, each ending with a guardian boss
  • Endless mode: how many waves can you survive?
  • Swarms in grids, chevrons, waves, diamonds and spinning rings
  • Power-ups: spread shot, shield, slow-time and extra lives
  • Up to 3 stars on every level
  • Glowing neon graphics and a synthwave soundtrack
  • Plays offline

Ads

Dronix is free to play. It shows a banner on the menus and occasional ads between levels, never during play. You can choose to watch an ad to continue after defeat or double a level's score.
