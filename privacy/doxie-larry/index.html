The Adventures Of Doxie Larry Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

The Adventures Of Doxie Larry (the "Game") is made by SchizoTroll Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles when you play it on an Android device.

In short

The Game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your progress is saved only on your device.

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Who is responsible

SchizoTroll Games, operated by Jason Fenech, Malta.
Contact: schizogamessupport@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves a few things locally, on your phone only:

  • which levels you have unlocked and your best score on each,
  • your sound setting,
  • whether you have bought the full game.

None of this leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your phone's settings.

3. Purchases

Levels 1 to 10 are free. The optional one-time "full game" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to that purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
